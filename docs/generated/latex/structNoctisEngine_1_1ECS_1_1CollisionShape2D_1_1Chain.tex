\doxysubsection{Detailed Description}
This is designed to eliminate ghost collisions with some limitations\+:
\begin{DoxyItemize}
\item Chains are one-\/sided
\item Chains have no mass and should be used on static bodies
\item Chains are either a loop or open
\item A chain must have at least 4 points
\item A chain shape should not self intersect (this is not validated)
\item An open chain shape has NO COLLISION on the first and final edge
\item You may overlap two open chains on their first three and/or last three points to get smooth collision 
\end{DoxyItemize}
